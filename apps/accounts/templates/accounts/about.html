About e-Citizen

Our Mission

e-Citizen is a digital platform that provides citizens with convenient access to government services online. Our mission is to simplify the process of accessing public services, making them more efficient, transparent, and accessible to everyone.

What We Offer

From applying for national identification documents to registering a business or filing taxes, e-Citizen brings essential government services to your fingertips. Our platform is designed to reduce paperwork, eliminate long queues, and provide real-time updates on your applications.

FAQs

How do I create an account?

Register through the onboarding page by clicking "Sign up" and following the on-screen instructions.

What services are available?

Browse our Services page to see all available government services including ID applications, permits, registrations, and more.

How can I track my application status?

Log in to your dashboard and visit the Applications page to see the current status of all your submissions.
