Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using e-Citizen

How do I create an e-Citizen account?

Click "Get Started" on the homepage, enter your national ID number and phone number, create a username and password, and verify your phone via SMS. The entire process takes about 2 minutes.

What services can I access on e-Citizen?

Over {{ stats.service_count|default:'100' }}+ services across national and county governments: national ID applications, passport applications, birth and death certificates, marriage certificates, business registration, land title searches, driving license applications, vehicle registration, NHIF registration, tax filing, HELB loan applications, and all county government services mandated by the Fourth Schedule of the Constitution.

How do I track my application status?

Log in to your dashboard and visit "My Applications." Each application shows its current status: Submitted, In Review, Pending Documents, Approved, or Rejected. You'll also receive SMS and email notifications at key milestones.

What payment methods are accepted?

We support M-Pesa (STK Push — instant), Visa and Mastercard debit/credit cards, and direct bank transfers. All payments are processed securely and you receive an electronic receipt immediately.

Is my personal data safe on e-Citizen?

Yes. e-Citizen complies with Kenya's Data Protection Act (2019). All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use two-factor authentication, biometric verification where applicable, and regular security audits. Your personal information is never shared with third parties without your explicit consent.

What is the difference between county and national services?

National government services include passports, national IDs, immigration, civil registration (birth/death/marriage certificates), and defense. County government services — defined by the Fourth Schedule of the Constitution — include health services, agriculture, county transport, trade licensing, county planning, pre-primary education, and more. Both are available on e-Citizen.

Can I use e-Citizen on my phone?

Absolutely. e-Citizen is fully responsive and works on any device — smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers. The platform is optimized for all mobile networks in Kenya, including 3G connections. You can complete entire application processes from your phone.

What do I do if I encounter a problem?

Visit our Contact Us page to send a message, call our support line at +254 700 000 000, or email support@ecitizen.go.ke. Support is available Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM EAT. You can also visit your nearest Huduma Centre for in-person assistance.
