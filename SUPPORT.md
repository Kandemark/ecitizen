# Support

Need help with e-Citizen? Here's where to go.

## For Citizens (Platform Users)

If you are a Kenyan citizen using the e-Citizen platform and need help with a service:

| Channel | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Phone** | +254 700 000 000 or toll-free 0800 000 000 |
| **Email** | support@ecitizen.go.ke |
| **Website** | [Contact Us](https://ecitizen.go.ke/contact/) |
| **In Person** | Visit any Huduma Centre nationwide |
| **USSD** | Dial *247# from any mobile phone |

**Working Hours:** Monday–Friday 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM EAT, Saturday 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM EAT. Closed Sundays and public holidays.

### Common Questions

- **How do I create an account?** Visit [ecitizen.go.ke](https://ecitizen.go.ke) and click "Get Started"
- **How do I track my application?** Log in and visit "My Applications" from your dashboard
- **How do I reset my password?** Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page
- **What payment methods are accepted?** M-Pesa, Visa, Mastercard, and bank transfers

### Reporting Security Issues

**Do not report security vulnerabilities in public issues.**

If you discover a security issue, please email **security@ecitizen.go.ke** with:

- A clear description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce
- Affected versions
- Any suggested fixes

We will acknowledge your report within 48 hours and provide a timeline for resolution. We practice responsible disclosure and will credit you in our release notes unless you request otherwise.

### Bug Reports

For non-security bugs, please open a GitHub issue with:

1. Your environment (Python version, Django version, browser, device)
2. Steps to reproduce the issue
3. What you expected to happen
4. What actually happened (include error messages and screenshots)

## For Government Staff

If you are a government officer using the Authorities dashboard:

- **Internal Support**: Contact your ministry's IT liaison officer
- **Training Materials**: Available through the Authorities portal help section
- **Service Configuration**: Managed by e-Citizen system administrators

## System Status

For information about planned maintenance, service disruptions, or system updates:

- Check the banner on the e-Citizen homepage
- Follow @eCitizenKenya on social media
- Subscribe to service notifications in your account settings
